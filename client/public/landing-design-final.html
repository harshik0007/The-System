A few things to know

Clear answers.
Room to begin.

What counts as a Task?

A Task is a specific action scheduled for a day, sized so you can get started without solving the whole goal at once.

What if I miss a Task?

If you attached a Backup plan, a Recovery Quest can give you a clear, time-bound way to return.

How do Ranks work?

Eligible completed Tasks earn XP. XP raises your Level, and Level gates open Ranks. Rank 01 is the highest.

Is my profile public?

Public profile visibility follows your privacy settings. You control what appears in community listings.
